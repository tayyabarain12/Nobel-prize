The Legacy of Alfred Nobel

The Nobel Prize was established by the will of Alfred Nobel, a Swedish chemist, engineer, and industrialist. Since 1901, it has honored men and women from all corners of the globe for outstanding achievements in physics, chemistry, medicine, literature, and for work in peace.

The Nobel Week Experience

Each December, the Nobel Prize laureates are honored during a week of festivities in Stockholm and Oslo. The week culminates on December 10, the anniversary of Alfred Nobel's death, with the prize award ceremonies and banquets. It is a week of dialogue, celebration, and inspiration.
