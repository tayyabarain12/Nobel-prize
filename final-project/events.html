Nobel Week 2024

Nobel Week is the highlight of the year, featuring the prestigious award ceremonies, laureate lectures, banquets, and cultural events in Stockholm and Oslo from December 6-10.

December 6-10, 2024

Join us in celebrating this year's laureates during a week of inspiring events and ceremonies

Watch Live

Event Schedule

DEC 6
Thursday

Laureates Arrive in Stockholm

The 2024 Nobel laureates arrive in Stockholm and Oslo for the beginning of Nobel Week festivities. Press conferences and initial meetings with the Nobel Foundation.

Stockholm & Oslo
DEC 7
Friday

Nobel Prize Lectures Begin

Laureates in Physics, Chemistry, and Economic Sciences present their prize lectures at Stockholm University and the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences.

09:00 - 17:00 | Stockholm University
DEC 8
Saturday

Medicine & Literature Lectures

Nobel laureates in Physiology or Medicine and Literature deliver their prize lectures. The Peace Prize lecture takes place in Oslo.

10:00 - 16:00 | Karolinska Institutet & Swedish Academy
DEC 10
Monday

Nobel Prize Award Ceremonies

The main event of Nobel Week! Award ceremonies take place simultaneously in Stockholm Concert Hall (Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Literature, Economic Sciences) and Oslo City Hall (Peace Prize).

Stockholm Ceremony: 16:30 CET

Location: Stockholm Concert Hall

Attendance: 1,300 guests including Swedish Royal Family

Broadcast: Live worldwide

Oslo Ceremony: 13:00 CET

Location: Oslo City Hall

Attendance: Norwegian Royal Family and dignitaries

DEC 10
Evening

Nobel Banquet

The prestigious Nobel Banquet at Stockholm City Hall features a specially designed menu, musical performances, and speeches by laureates. This black-tie event is attended by 1,300 guests.

19:00 | Stockholm City Hall | Formal Dress Code

Additional Events

Nobel Prize Museum

Special exhibitions featuring 2024 laureates, their discoveries, and the history of the Nobel Prize.

Open Daily

Student Events

Laureates meet with students and young researchers to inspire the next generation of scientists and thinkers.

December 7-9

Nobel Prize Concert

A special concert performed by world-class musicians in honor of the Nobel laureates.

December 8

Watch Live
